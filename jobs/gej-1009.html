Instrumentation & Control Engineer

Saudi Energy Ventures
📍 Jubail, Saudi Arabia 💼 Full-time ⏳ 5+ years
Oil & Gas Process & Facilities

About the role

Provide instrumentation and control engineering across projects and operations for a large gas-processing complex in Jubail.

Key responsibilities

  • Design and specify field instrumentation and control systems
  • Support DCS, PLC and SIS/ESD system engineering
  • Perform SIL assessments and loop checks
  • Review vendor documentation and support FAT/SAT
  • Troubleshoot control-system issues during operations

What you'll bring

  • BSc in Instrumentation / Electronics Engineering
  • 5+ years I&C experience in oil & gas
  • Knowledge of DCS/SIS (Honeywell, Yokogawa, Emerson)
  • Understanding of IEC 61511 functional safety
  • Strong documentation skills

What's on offer

  • Tax-free income
  • Compound housing
  • Medical cover
  • Annual bonus
