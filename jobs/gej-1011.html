Wind Energy Analyst

Masdar Clean Energy
📍 Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates 💼 Full-time ⏳ 3+ years 🌐 Remote friendly
Renewable Energy Renewables & Clean Energy

About the role

Support the development pipeline of wind projects across the Middle East and North Africa through resource assessment and energy-yield modelling.

Key responsibilities

  • Perform wind resource assessment and energy-yield analysis
  • Analyse met-mast and LiDAR data
  • Support turbine layout optimisation
  • Prepare technical inputs for bankable reports
  • Assess uncertainty and P50/P90 estimates

What you'll bring

  • Degree in Engineering, Meteorology or Physics
  • 3+ years wind resource / energy-yield experience
  • Proficiency with WindPRO, WAsP or openWind
  • Strong statistical and Python skills
  • Attention to detail

What's on offer

  • Tax-free salary
  • Hybrid / remote working
  • Medical insurance
  • Learning budget
