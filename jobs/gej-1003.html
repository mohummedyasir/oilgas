HSE Manager

Qatar LNG Solutions
📍 Ras Laffan, Qatar 💼 Full-time ⏳ 10+ years
LNG HSE & Environment Featured

About the role

We are looking for a seasoned HSE Manager to lead health, safety and environmental performance at one of Qatar's flagship LNG facilities. You will champion process safety and behavioural safety across a large multinational workforce.

Key responsibilities

  • Lead the site HSE management system and drive continuous improvement
  • Ensure compliance with Qatar regulations and international standards (ISO 45001, 14001)
  • Manage incident investigation, root-cause analysis and lessons learned
  • Deliver process safety management (PSM) programmes
  • Coach and develop the HSE team and site leadership

What you'll bring

  • Degree in Engineering, Occupational Health or related field
  • 10+ years HSE experience in oil & gas / LNG, 5+ in a leadership role
  • NEBOSH Diploma; process safety qualification preferred
  • Strong knowledge of PSM and major-accident hazard management
  • Proven ability to influence a diverse workforce

What's on offer

  • Tax-free package
  • Family status & housing
  • Medical & life insurance
  • Annual leave with flights
