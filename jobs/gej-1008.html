Electrical Engineer - Power Generation

Bahrain Power & Water
📍 Manama, Bahrain 💼 Full-time ⏳ 4+ years
Power Power & Utilities

About the role

Join a leading power and water utility to support the reliable operation of gas-fired combined-cycle plants and associated electrical systems.

Key responsibilities

  • Support O&M of HV/MV electrical systems and generators
  • Lead electrical fault diagnosis and reliability improvement
  • Manage protection systems and switchgear maintenance
  • Support outage planning and execution
  • Ensure compliance with grid code and safety standards

What you'll bring

  • BSc in Electrical Engineering
  • 4+ years experience in power generation or heavy industry
  • Knowledge of HV systems, protection relays and switchgear
  • Familiarity with combined-cycle plant operations
  • Strong troubleshooting skills

What's on offer

  • Tax-free salary
  • Housing allowance
  • Medical insurance
  • Training & development
