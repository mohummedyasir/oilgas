KRC

Process Engineer

Kuwait Refining Company
📍 Al Ahmadi, Kuwait 💼 Full-time ⏳ 4+ years
Refining & Petrochemicals Process & Facilities

About the role

Support the safe and efficient operation of refinery process units. You will monitor unit performance, troubleshoot process upsets and deliver optimisation and debottlenecking studies.

Key responsibilities

  • Monitor and optimise process unit performance and yields
  • Troubleshoot process upsets and lead root-cause investigations
  • Prepare process design packages and support MOC
  • Develop heat and material balances and simulation models
  • Support turnaround planning and commissioning

What you'll bring

  • BSc in Chemical Engineering
  • 4+ years process engineering experience in refining or petrochemicals
  • Proficiency with Aspen HYSYS / PRO/II
  • Knowledge of relief systems and process safety
  • Strong analytical and reporting skills

What's on offer

  • Tax-free salary
  • Accommodation provided
  • Transport allowance
  • Annual bonus
