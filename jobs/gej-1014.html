Mechanical Maintenance Engineer

Kuwait Refining Company
📍 Al Ahmadi, Kuwait 💼 Full-time ⏳ 5+ years
Refining & Petrochemicals Maintenance & Reliability

About the role

Improve the reliability and availability of critical refinery equipment through predictive and preventive maintenance strategies.

Key responsibilities

  • Lead reliability improvement for pumps, compressors and turbines
  • Develop preventive and predictive maintenance plans
  • Perform root-cause failure analysis (RCFA)
  • Support turnaround and shutdown execution
  • Manage spare-parts strategy and vendor support

What you'll bring

  • BSc in Mechanical Engineering
  • 5+ years maintenance experience in refining or petrochemicals
  • Knowledge of rotating equipment and vibration analysis
  • Familiarity with CMMS (SAP PM / Maximo)
  • Strong problem-solving skills

What's on offer

  • Tax-free salary
  • Accommodation provided
  • Medical insurance
  • Annual bonus
