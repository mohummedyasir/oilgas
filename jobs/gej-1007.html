Pipeline Integrity Engineer

Oman Gas Transmission
📍 Muscat, Oman 💼 Full-time ⏳ 5+ years
Oil & Gas Pipelines & Integrity

About the role

Safeguard the integrity of Oman's gas transmission network. You will lead pipeline integrity management, corrosion control and in-line inspection programmes.

Key responsibilities

  • Develop and implement pipeline integrity management systems (PIMS)
  • Plan and evaluate in-line inspection (ILI) runs
  • Lead corrosion management and cathodic protection programmes
  • Perform fitness-for-service and risk-based assessments
  • Manage repair and rehabilitation campaigns

What you'll bring

  • Degree in Mechanical / Materials Engineering
  • 5+ years pipeline integrity experience
  • Knowledge of ASME B31.8, API 1160 and corrosion mechanisms
  • NACE / AMPP certification preferred
  • Strong data-analysis skills

What's on offer

  • Tax-free income
  • Housing allowance
  • Family medical cover
  • Annual flights
