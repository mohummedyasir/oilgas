Rig Manager

Desert Drilling Contractors
📍 Dammam, Saudi Arabia 💼 Full-time ⏳ 12+ years
Oil & Gas Drilling & Wells

About the role

Lead all aspects of land rig operations, ensuring drilling programmes are delivered safely, efficiently and to client specification.

Key responsibilities

  • Manage day-to-day rig operations, crews and logistics
  • Own rig HSE performance and enforce a zero-harm culture
  • Liaise with the operator's drilling representatives
  • Manage rig maintenance, inventory and cost control
  • Ensure well-control readiness and competency

What you'll bring

  • 12+ years drilling operations experience, 3+ as Rig Manager / Toolpusher
  • IWCF Well Control (Supervisor level)
  • Strong knowledge of land rig equipment and operations
  • Excellent leadership and crew-management skills
  • Fluent English

What's on offer

  • Tax-free salary
  • Rotational schedule
  • Camp accommodation
  • Completion bonus
